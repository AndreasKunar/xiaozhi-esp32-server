# Open Letter to Developers

> “The warm spring river makes ducks aware first; it’s just when the sturgeon is about to rise!”

Dear friend, I am John, an ordinary Java programmer at a company, and today I am writing to you with the utmost sincerity.  

Half a year ago I saw many excellent projects, such as `Dify` and `Chat2DB`, which are AI‑related. I thought it would be wonderful to take part in them, but unfortunately I have no opportunity to contribute to my country—only to write code for ten years.

I came across a video by the Xiong Ge team at the beginning of 2025 and became very curious about how they achieved it. I wanted to replicate their backend service and build a low‑cost civilian JARVIS. Unfortunately, what I have now is merely an “artificial idiot”: it has low concurrency, lacks soul, responds slowly, and contains many bugs.  

The Xiong Ge team is our role model, and I would love to have an intelligent backend service like theirs. Yet I also understand why Xiong Ge chose not to open‑source their work.  

> “A single flower blooming does not herald spring; a hundred flowers in full bloom fill the garden.”  
> In an era where AI blossoms everywhere, perhaps our generation will finally realize it, and we can build a low‑cost civilian JARVIS with our own hands. I call this **our pilgrimage**.  

So, what difficulties will we encounter on this pilgrimage? I imagine there will be no fewer than eighty‑one trials. Along the way, various demons will inevitably appear, but there will also be immortals secretly assisting us, and others will join the pilgrimage.  

If you find the above amusing, I consider myself extremely lucky. To be able to make you smile for five seconds in the 30,000‑plus days of your life feels like a small contribution on my part.  

Will the idea of a low‑cost civilian JARVIS fail? I don’t know, but isn’t failure a common part of ordinary people’s lives?  

In the future, one thing is certain: someone will completely replicate the Xiong Ge team’s functionality to achieve a low‑cost civilian JARVIS. Perhaps that someone will be us?  

I look forward to joining forces with you and creating the future together.  

John, March 11, 2025, Guangzhou

# Contributor Guide  

## Project Goals  

1. **Low‑Cost Civilian JARVIS Solution**  
2. **Intelligent Peripheral Hardware Integration Solution**  

## Join Us  

We sincerely welcome like‑minded friends to join and contribute to the project. You can view our upcoming features that still lack assigned developers at [this link](https://github.com/users/xinnan-tech/projects/3). Features in the list that have not been assigned are precisely where your participation is needed. Participation methods are as follows:  

### 1. Become an Ordinary Contributor  

- Fork the project.  
- Submit a Pull Request (PR).  
- After review by developers, the PR will be merged into the main branch.  

### 2. Become a Developer  

- After accumulating **3 valid PRs**, you may contact the group owner to become a developer.  
- The owner will then invite you to an independent developer group to discuss the project’s future.  

## Developer Workflow  

1. **Create a New Branch**  
   - For each feature, develop on a new branch.  
   - The branch name should be concise and clearly indicate the implemented functionality, avoiding feature collisions.  

2. **Submit PR for Review**  
   - Once the feature is developed, submit the PR on GitHub.  
   - The PR will be reviewed by other developers; upon approval, it will be merged into the main branch.
